Before you start: In MELD Studio, use File → Export Session to save each of the two sessions as a .json file. Toolbox creates a new session without changing either original.

1 · Choose two session files

2 · Choose scenes to copy

Toolbox also includes scenes that your selected scenes need. If a scene already exists, choose what to do with it. Keep Both is the default.

Keep Both: Make a separate copy of the scene.
Merge: Add different layers to the existing scene.
Overwrite: Replace the existing scene’s contents.
Skip: Keep the existing scene as it is.
